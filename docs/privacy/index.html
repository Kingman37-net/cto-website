Information may be used for legitimate professional and operational purposes, including:

Depending on the person, organization, service, transaction, location, and nature of the processing, applicable requirements may include laws and regulations in jurisdictions such as:

  • The United Kingdom, including applicable UK data-protection and privacy requirements.

  • The United States, including applicable federal and state privacy laws where relevant.
  • Mexico, including applicable private-sector privacy requirements.
