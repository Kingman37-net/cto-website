This policy explains the use of cookies and browser-based storage technologies on the CTO KINGMAN KE website.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small pieces of information stored by a website in a browser. Websites may also use other browser technologies, such as local storage, for certain technical functions or preferences.

2. Current Website Storage

The current website uses browser local storage to remember the visual theme selected by the visitor.

Storage key: cto-theme
Purpose: remembers dark/light theme preference
Storage type: browser local storage

This theme preference is not an HTTP cookie. It is stored locally by the visitor's browser and is used to preserve the selected visual preference on subsequent visits.

3. Cookies Currently Used by This Website

Based on the current website implementation, the site does not intentionally create a first-party session cookie or analytics cookie through its own JavaScript.

Third-party services may use their own technologies when you interact with them after following an external link.

4. Third-Party Services

The website links to external platforms such as GitHub, LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook, X, Telegram, WhatsApp, and the broader KDCN ecosystem.

Those services operate independently and may use cookies, local storage, tracking technologies, or other mechanisms according to their own policies.

5. Managing Browser Storage

Visitors can generally manage cookies and browser storage through the settings of their web browser.

Clearing browser storage may remove the saved cto-theme preference, meaning the website may return to its default visual theme.

6. Future Changes

If analytics, advertising, authentication, forms, embedded services, or other technologies that use cookies or similar mechanisms are introduced, this policy should be reviewed and updated to reflect the actual implementation.
