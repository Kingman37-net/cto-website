7. CONTENT PRINCIPLES

The public CTO website should follow these principles:

1. Professional substance over personal publicity.


2. Clear professional identity.


3. Minimal unnecessary private information.


4. No private life-story material unless specifically approved for publication.


5. No duplication of the KDCN project catalogue.


6. No unsupported claims added simply to increase search visibility.


7. Keep information useful, accurate, understandable, and maintainable.


8. Let professional work and documentation establish credibility.
